## Explicit Non-Goals

### 1. We are not building a pure GTA clone
We do not prioritise chaos, shooting galleries, or mission-based crime sprees as the core loop. Crime exists as one system among many, **with consequences** — not as the main fantasy.

### 2. We are not building The Sims in 3D
We are not primarily a single-player dollhouse or needs-management game. Needs create **pressure and routine**, not the entire game.

### 3. We are not building a menu / tap-based life simulator
Lagos Life-style map-tapping and pure UI management is **explicitly rejected** as the primary interaction model. **Physical presence and movement are mandatory.**

### 4. We are not making NPCs the main population
NPCs are **support systems only**. Designing the economy, social life, or city activity primarily around NPCs is forbidden.

### 5. We are not targeting ultra-realistic graphics
Visual fidelity is always secondary to **mobile performance, clarity, and art direction** that serves the fantasy. Photorealism is a non-goal.

### 6. We are not building a hardcore simulation of real Nigerian law or real crime
All illegal activities are **fictional game mechanics**. We provide no real-world criminal instruction.

### 7. We are not building a single-player narrative campaign as the main product
Story **emerges from systems and players**, not from a long authored single-player plot.

### 8. We are not optimising first for high-end PC or console
**Mobile (mid-range Android) is the primary design target.** Other platforms may come later; they do not drive design decisions.

### 9. We are not creating a pay-to-win economy
Monetisation must never destroy the **fairness of the player-driven economy** or progression.

### 10. We are not allowing unrestricted real-money trading of game assets at launch
Any future real-money features will be **carefully controlled** and must not undermine the internal economy.

## How to Use This Document

Any proposed feature that moves the project toward a Non-Goal must be **rejected**, or require a **formal ADR** that explicitly changes this document. Silence is not consent.
