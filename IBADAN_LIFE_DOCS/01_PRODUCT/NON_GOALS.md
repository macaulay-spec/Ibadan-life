# NON-GOALS – IBADAN LIFE

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active (Tier 0)  
**Owner:** Design Authority  

This document is as important as the goals. It protects the project from scope creep and identity dilution.

---

## Explicit Non-Goals

### 1. We are not building a pure GTA clone
- We do not prioritise pure chaos, shooting galleries, or mission-based crime sprees as the core loop.
- Crime exists as one system among many, with consequences, not as the main fantasy.

### 2. We are not building The Sims in 3D
- We are not primarily a single-player dollhouse or needs-management game.
- Needs exist to create pressure and routine, not to be the entire game.

### 3. We are not building a menu / tap-based life simulator
- Lagos Life-style map tapping and pure UI management is explicitly rejected as the primary interaction model.
- Physical presence and movement are mandatory.

### 4. We are not making NPCs the main population
- NPCs are support systems only.
- Designing the economy, social life, or city activity primarily around NPCs is forbidden.

### 5. We are not targeting ultra-realistic graphics
- Visual fidelity will always be secondary to mobile performance, clarity, and art direction that serves the fantasy.
- Photorealism is a non-goal.

### 6. We are not building a hardcore simulation of real Nigerian law or real crime
- All illegal activities are fictional game mechanics.
- We do not provide real-world criminal instruction.

### 7. We are not building a single-player narrative campaign as the main product
- Story emerges from systems and players, not from a long authored single-player plot.

### 8. We are not optimising first for high-end PC or console
- Mobile (especially mid-range Android) is the primary design target.
- Other platforms may come later; they do not drive design decisions.

### 9. We are not creating a pay-to-win economy
- Monetisation must not destroy the fairness of the player-driven economy or progression.

### 10. We are not allowing unrestricted real-money trading of game assets at launch
- Any future real-money features will be carefully controlled and must not undermine the internal economy.

---

## How to Use This Document

Any proposed feature that moves the project toward a Non-Goal must be rejected or require a formal ADR that explicitly changes this document.
