# PROJECT GOALS – IBADAN LIFE

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active (Tier 0)  

---

## Primary Goals

1. Deliver a persistent 3D open-world multiplayer experience in which real players form the living population of a city inspired by Ibadan.
2. Create a player-driven economy in which businesses, jobs, trade, and services are primarily sustained by real players.
3. Provide genuine third-person physical freedom of movement and interaction inside a continuous city.
4. Support believable long-term life progression (housing, work, relationships, reputation, business ownership).
5. Achieve stable, enjoyable performance on mid-range Android devices.
6. Maintain cultural authenticity and respect for Nigerian (specifically Ibadan) urban life.
7. Build the technical and operational foundation for a live-service game that can grow over years.

---

## Secondary Goals

- Support both legal and fictional illegal gameplay systems with meaningful consequences.
- Create strong social tools that encourage real relationships and communities.
- Enable players to leave lasting marks on the city (businesses, property, reputation).
- Design systems that remain interesting after hundreds of hours.
- Establish clear, maintainable architecture that multiple AI agents and future human developers can work within safely.

---

## Non-Quantified Aspirational Goals

- Become the definitive digital Nigerian city experience.
- Foster genuine player cultures, traditions, and social norms inside the game.
- Create stories that players tell each other outside the game.
