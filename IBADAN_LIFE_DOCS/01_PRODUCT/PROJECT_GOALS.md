# PROJECT GOALS – IBADAN LIFE

**Version:** 2.0  
**Last Updated:** October 2026  
**Status:** Active  
**Owner:** Project Lead  
**Tier:** 0  
**Depends On:** PROJECT_CHARTER.md, PRODUCT_VISION.md  

> **North Star:** what we are building toward — primary, secondary, and aspirational.

---

## Primary Goals

1. Deliver a **persistent 3D open-world multiplayer** experience where real players form the living population of a city inspired by Ibadan.
2. Create a **player-driven economy** in which businesses, jobs, trade, and services are sustained primarily by real players.
3. Provide genuine **third-person physical freedom** of movement and interaction inside a continuous city.
4. Support **believable long-term life progression** — housing, work, relationships, reputation, business ownership.
5. Achieve **stable, enjoyable performance on mid-range Android** devices.
6. Maintain **cultural authenticity** and respect for Nigerian (specifically Ibadan) urban life.
7. Build the **technical and operational foundation** for a live-service game that grows for years.

---

## Secondary Goals

- Support both **legal and fictional illegal** gameplay with meaningful consequences.
- Create **strong social tools** that encourage real relationships and communities.
- Let players **leave lasting marks** on the city — businesses, property, reputation.
- Design systems that stay interesting **after hundreds of hours**.
- Keep a **clear, maintainable architecture** that many AI agents and future developers can work within safely.

---

## Non-Quantified Aspirational Goals

- Become the **definitive digital Nigerian city** experience.
- Foster genuine **player cultures, traditions, and social norms** inside the game.
- Create **stories players tell each other** outside the game.
- Be a game that makes Nigeria **proud**.

---

## How Goals Connect to Metrics

Every primary goal maps to measurable Key Results in **SUCCESS_METRICS.md**. If a goal cannot be measured, it is not yet a goal — it is a wish.
