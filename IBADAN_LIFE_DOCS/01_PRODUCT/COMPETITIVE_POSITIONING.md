## Positioning Statement

For **Nigerian and African players who want to live a second life in a digital city that feels like home**, IBADAN LIFE is the **persistent 3D multiplayer life simulation** that combines real physical freedom of movement with a player-driven society and economy — unlike menu-based life sims or generic open-world action games.

---

## The Category We Create

> **The Interdependent Urban Simulation.**

A persistent 3D city where real players are the population, the economy, and the story.

---

| Dimension | Lagos Life / Menu Sims | Typical GTA-style Games | **IBADAN LIFE** |

---

## What We Learned From the Market

**Lagos Life** proved the demand: **3M+ registered accounts** and **peak CCU above 100,000** within days. But first-generation browser titles suffer real bottlenecks — client-side state exploits, text-centric interfaces, static loops, non-spatial socialising. **IBADAN LIFE is the answer to all four:** a 3D spatial world, server-authoritative state, physical presence, and proximity-based social design.

---

## Competitive Moat We Aim to Build

1. **Authentic sense of place** — Ibadan texture you cannot fake.
2. **Genuine player interdependence** in the economy.
3. **The technical ability** to run a persistent 3D city on the devices our audience owns.
4. **Cultural trust** and community ownership.

---

## What We Explicitly Refuse to Compete On

- Pure graphical fidelity against AAA Western titles
- Pure chaotic crime sandbox as the main fantasy
- Browser-only ultra-lightweight experience
- Whale-driven pay-to-win economies
