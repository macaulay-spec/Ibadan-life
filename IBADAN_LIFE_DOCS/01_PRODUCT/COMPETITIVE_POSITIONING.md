# COMPETITIVE POSITIONING – IBADAN LIFE

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  

---

## Positioning Statement

For Nigerian and African players who want to live a second life in a digital city that feels like home,  
IBADAN LIFE is the persistent 3D multiplayer life simulation  
that combines real physical freedom of movement with a player-driven society and economy,  
unlike menu-based life sims or generic open-world action games.

---

## Key Differentiators

| Dimension                    | Lagos Life / Menu Sims      | Typical GTA-style Games     | IBADAN LIFE                          |
|-----------------------------|-----------------------------|-----------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Movement & Presence         | Tap / menu                  | Strong                      | Strong (core pillar)                 |
| Population                  | Mixed / light multiplayer   | Mostly NPC + some players   | Real players primary                 |
| Economy                     | System-driven               | Mission / activity driven   | Player-to-player interdependent      |
| Cultural Specificity        | Strong (Lagos)              | Usually generic or Western  | Strong (Ibadan-specific)             |
| Life Simulation Depth       | High                        | Low                         | High                                 |
| Platform Focus              | Browser / light mobile      | Console / high-end          | Mobile-first (mid-range Android)     |
| Persistence of Society      | Limited                     | Session or server based     | Persistent player-driven city        |

---

## Competitive Moat We Aim to Build

1. Authentic sense of place (Ibadan texture)
2. Genuine player interdependence in the economy
3. Technical ability to run a persistent 3D city on the devices our audience owns
4. Cultural trust and community ownership

---

## What We Explicitly Refuse to Compete On

- Pure graphical fidelity against AAA Western titles
- Pure chaotic crime sandbox as the main fantasy
- Browser-only ultra-lightweight experience
