# TARGET AUDIENCE – IBADAN LIFE

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  

---

## Primary Audience

**Nigerian urban youth and young adults (16–35)** who:
- Own mid-range Android smartphones
- Are familiar with or curious about life simulation and open-world games
- Have experienced or heard of Lagos Life or similar cultural games
- Want a digital space that reflects their reality, humour, aspirations, and environment
- Value social status, hustle, relationships, and visible progress

---

## Secondary Audiences

- Nigerian diaspora who want a connection to home
- Other West African players
- Global players interested in culturally specific multiplayer experiences

---

## Audience Insights (Design Implications)

- Data costs and device performance matter enormously → aggressive optimisation and smart streaming.
- Social visibility and status are powerful motivators → reputation, property, vehicles, and businesses must be visible.
- Many players will play in short sessions → systems must support meaningful progress in 20–40 minute sessions.
- Trust and safety are critical in a player-driven social economy → strong moderation and reporting tools from day one.
- Cultural humour and recognition create strong emotional attachment → authenticity is a retention feature.

---

## Out of Primary Scope

- Hardcore Western open-world action players looking for pure GTA-style chaos
- Players who only want single-player narrative experiences
- High-end PC-only audiences as the design driver
