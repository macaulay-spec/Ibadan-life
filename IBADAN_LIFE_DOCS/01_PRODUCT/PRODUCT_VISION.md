# PRODUCT VISION – IBADAN LIFE

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active (Tier 0)  
**Owner:** Project Lead / Design Authority  

---

## Vision Statement

We are building a persistent digital city inspired by Ibadan where real people live second lives.

Players do not log in to complete quests or manage menus.  
They log in to exist — to walk the streets, find a place to sleep, earn money, spend money, meet people, start businesses, build reputations, fall in love, get into trouble, and create stories that belong to them.

The city is not a backdrop. It is a society.

---

## The Fantasy We Sell

“I have a life in Ibadan that continues even when I am offline.  
I have a home, a job or a business, friends, enemies, status, and freedom to move through a real 3D city.  
Other real people depend on me and I depend on them.”

---

## Core Experience Pillars (Summary)

1. **Physical Presence** – You control a real 3D body in a continuous world.
2. **Player-First Population** – Real humans are the main inhabitants.
3. **Interconnected Player Economy** – Players create value for other players.
4. **Authentic Ibadan Texture** – The city feels specifically like Ibadan, not a generic African city or a Lagos copy.
5. **Believable Life Progression** – From arrival to established resident through natural systems.
6. **Mobile-First Reality** – Designed from the ground up to run well on the devices our audience actually owns.

---

## Long-Term Ambition

To become the definitive persistent multiplayer Nigerian digital city — a place where hundreds of thousands of players maintain ongoing lives, businesses, and social circles, and where the culture, humour, struggles, and aspirations of contemporary Nigerian urban life are reflected with respect and authenticity.

---

## What Success Feels Like for a Player

- “I know my way around this city.”
- “I have a routine.”
- “People recognise me.”
- “My business needs other players to succeed.”
- “I got into trouble and had to deal with the consequences.”
- “This place feels like home.”
