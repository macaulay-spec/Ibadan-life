# DESIGN PILLARS – IBADAN LIFE

**Version:** 2.0  
**Last Updated:** October 2026  
**Status:** Active  
**Owner:** Design Authority  
**Tier:** 0  
**Depends On:** PRODUCT_VISION.md, PROJECT_CHARTER.md  

> **North Star:** six non-negotiable pillars. Every feature must serve at least one and undermine none.

---

## 1. Physical Presence

The player controls a fully realised 3D character in continuous space. Walking, running, driving, entering buildings, and interacting with the world must feel **tangible**. The city is not a menu or a map of icons — it is a place you move through **with your body**.

**Test:** Can the player feel the weight and freedom of existing inside the world?

---

## 2. Player-First Population

**Real human players are the primary population** of the city. NPCs exist only for essential services, ambient life, traffic, police, and specific world functions. Social life, economic activity, and meaningful relationships are designed around real players.

If you dine at an amala joint, the owner, the cook, the driver who delivered the yams, and the patrons are **real players**. NPCs only do what humans cannot reliably be online to do.

**Test:** If all NPCs disappeared, would the city still feel alive with players?

---

## 3. Interconnected Player Economy

Players create value for other players. One player owns a restaurant, another works there, another buys food, another delivers, another supplies ingredients, another advertises. The economy is a **web of interdependence**, not isolated PvE money faucets.

**Test:** Does this system create meaningful dependency between players?

---

## 4. Authentic Ibadan Texture

The world, culture, humour, architecture, transport, social norms, and daily rhythms must feel **specifically like Ibadan** — and broader contemporary Nigerian urban life. Not a generic "African city." Not a Lagos clone. Cultural authenticity is a **design requirement**, not decoration.

**Test:** Would someone who knows Ibadan recognise the soul of the place?

---

## 5. Believable Life Progression

Players progress through natural life systems: **arrival → shelter → income → spending → skills & relationships → better housing/vehicles → business ownership → reputation → deeper civic and economic participation.**

Progression feels **earned and continuous**, not gated behind arbitrary levels or battle-pass trackers.

**Test:** Does the player's story feel like a life, or like a checklist?

---

## 6. Mobile-First Reality

Every design decision respects the constraints and opportunities of **mid-range Android devices** common in our market. Performance, battery, thermal, network, and touch controls are **first-class concerns from day one**. We do not design for high-end PC and "port down."

**Test:** Does this feature remain enjoyable and stable on a typical mid-range Android phone?

---

## Usage Rule

When evaluating any proposed feature or system, ask:

1. **Which pillars does it strengthen?**
2. **Does it weaken any pillar?**
3. If it weakens a pillar, is the trade-off **explicitly accepted via ADR**?
