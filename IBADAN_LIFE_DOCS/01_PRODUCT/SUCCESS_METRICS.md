> **North Star:** how we know the player-driven society is actually forming — not just that people downloaded the game.

---

## North Star Metric

> **Weekly Active Players who performed at least one meaningful economic or social action with another real player.**

This is the single number that tells us the city is alive. Everything else supports it.

---

## Key Result Areas

### 1. Retention
- Day 1 / Day 7 / Day 30 retention
- % of players who return in week 4 and still have an active home or income source

### 2. Social & Economy Health *(the North Star in detail)*
- % of active players who interacted with another real player in the last 7 days
- Number of **player-owned businesses** that earned revenue from other players
- Average **player-to-player transactions** per active player per week
- % of jobs filled by real players vs. NPC fallback

### 3. Presence & Engagement
- Average session length
- Average sessions per week
- % of playtime in **free movement / exploration** vs. menus
- Number of unique **districts visited** per player

### 4. Progression Health
- % of players reaching **"stable housing + regular income"** within the first 10 hours
- % of players who own a **vehicle or business** by day 30
- Distribution of reputation and wealth (must not be extremely top-heavy early)

### 5. Technical Health
- Crash-free session rate
- Average **FPS on target mid-range devices**
- Server tick and replication performance
- Average load times and streaming hitches

### 6. Cultural & Qualitative
- Player sentiment on authenticity ("**feels like Ibadan / Nigeria**")
- Organic **player stories** and community formation
- Moderation incident rate

---

## Leading Indicators (Pre-Launch)

- Vertical Slice completion rate and qualitative feedback
- Closed-beta retention and player-to-player interaction rates
- Economy simulation stability under load

---

## Anti-Metrics (What We Will Not Chase)

- Pure download counts (vanity)
- Revenue per user at the cost of fairness (P2W)
- Session length gained through anxiety loops
- Engagement bought with dark patterns

We optimise for **a city people love**, not a dashboard people inflate.
