# SUCCESS METRICS – IBADAN LIFE

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  

---

## North Star Metric

**Weekly Active Players who have performed at least one meaningful economic or social action with another real player.**

This measures whether the player-driven society is actually forming.

---

## Key Result Areas

### 1. Retention
- Day 1 Retention
- Day 7 Retention
- Day 30 Retention
- Percentage of players who return in week 4 and still have an active home or income source

### 2. Social & Economy Health
- Percentage of active players who interacted with another real player in the last 7 days
- Number of player-owned businesses that generated revenue from other players
- Average number of player-to-player transactions per active player per week
- Percentage of jobs filled by real players vs temporary NPC fallback

### 3. Presence & Engagement
- Average session length
- Average sessions per week
- Percentage of playtime spent in free movement / exploration vs menus
- Number of unique districts visited per player

### 4. Progression Health
- Percentage of players who reach “stable housing + regular income” within first 10 hours
- Percentage of players who own a vehicle or business by day 30
- Distribution of reputation and wealth (should not be extremely top-heavy early)

### 5. Technical Health
- Crash-free session rate
- Average FPS on target mid-range devices
- Server tick and replication performance
- Average load times and streaming hitches

### 6. Cultural & Qualitative
- Player sentiment regarding authenticity (“feels like Ibadan / Nigeria”)
- Organic player stories and community formation
- Moderation incident rate

---

## Leading Indicators (Pre-Launch)

- Vertical Slice completion rate and qualitative feedback
- Closed beta retention and player-to-player interaction rates
- Economy simulation stability under load
