> **North Star:** progression that feels like a life — earned, continuous, and never a checklist.

---

## 1. Progression Philosophy

Progression in IBADAN LIFE is **not** XP bars and level-ups. It is **a life getting better**: a worse room to a better room, a hustle to a business, a stranger to a name.

**Rules:**
- **Earned, never gated.** Progress comes from doing, not from waiting on a timer.
- **Continuous, never arbitrary.** No battle-pass tracks; no level gates that stop the story.
- **Visible.** Others can see your progress — your home, your car, your shop, your name.
- **Multi-path.** Many ways to rise; no single "correct" build.

---

## 2. The Progression Axes

A player grows along **five axes**, each independent and each visible:

| Axis | What grows | How it shows |
|------|-----------|--------------|
| **Wealth** | Naira, assets, property | Home, car, shop, clothes |
| **Skill** | Career ability, craft, hustle | Better jobs, better pay, better deals |
| **Reputation** | Standing in districts & circles | Recognition, trust, access |
| **Relationships** | Friends, crew, family, rivals | Who knows you, who owes you |
| **Influence** | Ownership, businesses, civic role | Jobs you give, deals you set, streets you shape |

---

## 3. The Life Progression Spine

**Arrive → Shelter → Income → Spend → Skills & Relationships → Better Assets → Business → Reputation → Legacy**

Each step **unlocks the next naturally**. You need shelter to rest well; rest to work well; work to earn; earn to buy; buy to build; build to be known.

---

## 4. Backgrounds (Starting Conditions)

| Background | Starting point | The story |
|------------|----------------|-----------|
| **Nepo** | Starting capital, some assets, connections | Born into ease — but expectations are higher |
| **Lapo** | Microfinance debt, hustle from zero, raw drive | Born into struggle — the climb is the story |

Both are **valid, respected paths**. Neither is mocked; neither is glorified beyond its reality.

---

## 5. Skill & Attribute Growth

- Skills improve **by doing** (work a shift → better at the job).
- Attributes (e.g., stamina, charisma, craft) shape what you can do and how well.
- Growth is **diminishing but never capped hard** — mastery is a horizon, not a wall.

*(Full system: SKILL_AND_ATTRIBUTE_SYSTEM.md)*

---

## 6. Reputation Growth

- Reputation is **district- and circle-specific** — famous in Dugbe, unknown in Bodija.
- It is earned through **consistent action**: pay debts, keep promises, show up, deliver.
- It unlocks **access**: better jobs, better housing, bigger deals, deeper trust.

*(Full system: REPUTATION_SYSTEM.md)*

---

## 7. Anti-Progression (What We Avoid)

- ❌ Pay-to-win: money must never buy **power**, only **cosmetics and convenience**.
- ❌ Grind walls: no "play 100 hours to unlock basic housing."
- ❌ Prestige resets that erase identity.
- ❌ A single meta: many viable paths to a good life.

---

## 8. Measuring Progression Health

- % of players reaching **stable housing + income** within 10 hours.
- % owning a **vehicle or business** by day 30.
- **Distribution** of wealth/reputation (must not be extremely top-heavy early).
- Player sentiment: *"my story feels like a life."*
