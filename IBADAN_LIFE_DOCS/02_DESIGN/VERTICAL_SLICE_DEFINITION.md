> **North Star:** a small, complete, high-quality playable experience that proves the core fantasy and the technical foundation before full production scales.

---

## Purpose

The Vertical Slice is **not a prototype of isolated systems**. It is a **miniature of the intended game** in one limited but fully realised space.

---

## Vertical Slice Goals

1. Prove third-person movement and presence **feel good on mobile**.
2. Prove a small piece of the city can **feel authentic and alive**.
3. Prove **real players can be the primary population** in a shared space.
4. Prove a basic **economic and survival loop** works.
5. Prove the **technical stack** (Unreal mobile + backend + persistence + replication) supports the vision at small scale.
6. Set a **concrete quality bar** for the team and future AI agents.

---

## Vertical Slice Scope

**Location:** One tightly designed area inspired by a recognisable part of Ibadan — recommended: the **UI Student Hub** and the **Saronda corridor** (academic + residential/commercial edge).

**Must Include:**
- Character creation → drop into the slice
- Full third-person controller + camera **polished for touch**
- **Day/night cycle**
- Basic **needs**
- At least one **functional housing** option
- **2–3 ways to earn money** that can involve other players
- Basic **shops / vendors** (player or supported)
- Other **real players visible and interactable**
- Simple **text communication**
- **Persistent save** of character state
- Acceptable **performance on target devices**

**Quality Bar:**
- Art and audio already feel **intentional and culturally grounded** (not greybox).
- Movement and camera feel good enough that people **want to walk around**.
- The space feels like a place people **want to return to**.

---

## Exit Criteria

- Internal and external testers can play **30–60 minutes** and describe the fantasy correctly.
- Technical metrics (**FPS, memory, network, crash rate**) are within acceptable early ranges.
- Design and engineering agree the foundations are **solid enough to expand**.
