# VERTICAL SLICE DEFINITION – IBADAN LIFE

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active (Tier 1)  

---

## Purpose

The Vertical Slice is a small, complete, high-quality playable experience that proves the core fantasy and technical foundations before full production scales.

It is not a prototype of isolated systems.  
It is a miniature version of the intended game experience in one limited but fully realised space.

---

## Vertical Slice Goals

1. Prove that third-person movement and presence feel good on mobile.
2. Prove that a small piece of the city can feel authentic and alive.
3. Prove that real players can be the primary population in a shared space.
4. Prove that a basic economic and survival loop works.
5. Prove that the technical stack (Unreal mobile + backend + persistence + replication) can support the vision at small scale.
6. Give the team and future AI agents a concrete quality bar.

---

## Vertical Slice Scope (Recommended)

**Location:** One tightly designed area inspired by a recognisable part of Ibadan (example candidates: area around University of Ibadan + nearby residential/commercial edge, or a focused Dugbe/Bodija-inspired commercial + residential pocket).

**Must Include:**
- Character creation → drop into the slice
- Full third-person controller + camera polished for touch
- Day/night cycle
- Basic needs
- At least one functional housing option
- At least 2–3 ways to earn money that can involve other players
- Basic shops / vendors (player or supported)
- Other real players visible and interactable
- Simple text communication
- Persistent save of character state
- Acceptable performance on target devices

**Quality Bar:**
- Art and audio should already feel intentional and culturally grounded (not greybox only).
- Movement and camera must feel good enough that people want to walk around.
- The space must feel like a place people would want to return to.

---

## Exit Criteria for Vertical Slice

- Internal and external testers can play for 30–60 minutes and describe the fantasy correctly.
- Technical metrics (FPS, memory, network, crash rate) are within acceptable early ranges.
- Design and engineering agree the foundations are solid enough to expand.
