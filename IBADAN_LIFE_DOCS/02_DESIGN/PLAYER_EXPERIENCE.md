> **North Star:** what it *feels* like to be a player in IBADAN LIFE — moment to moment, session to session.

---

## 1. The Feeling We Design For

A player should feel, within minutes:

- **I am really here.** My body, my streets, my city.
- **I matter.** People notice me; my work changes things.
- **I belong.** I have a place, a routine, people who know me.

---

## 2. First 10 Minutes (The Hook)

1. **Create** — pick a background (Nepo or Lapo), shape your face and clothes.
2. **Arrive** — step into the UI Student Hub. Day or night, the city is already moving.
3. **Move** — walk, run, ride. The controls answer your thumb.
4. **Notice** — other real players are everywhere. Some wave.
5. **Act** — buy your first plate of food, or take your first small job.
6. **Earn** — your first Naira. It is yours.

By minute ten, the player has **moved, eaten, earned, and seen another human**. That is the fantasy, delivered fast.

---

## 3. The Emotional Arc of a Session

| Phase | Feeling | What the game does |
|-------|---------|--------------------|
| **Arrival** | Curiosity | A vivid, alive street; clear first step |
| **Orientation** | Competence | Controls respond; goals are clear |
| **Hustle** | Agency | Work, earn, choose what to buy |
| **Connection** | Belonging | Meet, chat, be recognised |
| **Stakes** | Tension | Needs, debt, wanted level, competition |
| **Payoff** | Pride | A better home, a vehicle, a business, a name |
| **Exit** | Satisfaction | Log off at home; the city continues without you |

---

## 4. Presence (Feeling Others)

- Other players are **visible, named, and nearby** — not icons on a map.
- **Proximity** governs interaction: you hear and see who is close.
- Reputation is visible: people **know** the hustlers, the troublemakers, the owners.
- The city **remembers**: your shop stays open, your home keeps your things, your name travels.

---

## 5. Agency (Feeling in Control)

- Players choose their **path**: worker, hustler, owner, driver, creator.
- Players choose their **pace**: a 20-minute errand or a 2-hour session.
- Players choose their **risk**: honest work, side hustles, or the underworld — each with consequences.
- **No forced builds.** Systems reward many kinds of lives.

---

## 6. Delight (The Moments We Craft)

- The first time another player recognises your name.
- The first Naira earned from a business *you* built.
- Driving your first car through Dugbe at night.
- Winning a deal, losing a deal, making it back.
- Coming home to a place that is yours.

---

## 7. Friction (Handled With Care)

| Friction | Our response |
|----------|--------------|
| Death / loss | Recovery paths, never permanent deletion |
| Debt | Work it off; consequences, not game-over |
| Griefing | Moderation, consequences, safe spaces |
| Confusion | Clear goals, good onboarding, helpful NPC services |
| Performance | Mobile-first; smooth on mid-range devices |

---

## 8. Experience Principles

1. **The city is the interface.** Minimise menus; maximise world.
2. **Every action has a visible consequence.**
3. **Other players are the content.**
4. **Short sessions must feel complete.**
5. **Failure is a story, not a dead end.**
