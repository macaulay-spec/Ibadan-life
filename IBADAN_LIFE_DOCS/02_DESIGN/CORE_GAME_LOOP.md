> **North Star:** nested loops that keep a player feeling the city, earning their life, and needing other people — at every timescale.

---

## 1. Philosophy

The core loop must continuously reinforce:

- **Physical presence** in the city
- **Interaction with other real players**
- **Economic and social interdependence**
- **Believable daily and long-term progression**

It must never collapse into pure menu management or isolated single-player activity.

---

## 2. The Loop Hierarchy (Four Timescales)

### 30-Second Loop (Moment-to-Moment)
Spatial navigation, avoiding traffic, scanning nearby players, catching proximity chat bubbles, feeling the street.

### 5-Minute Loop (Immediate Needs)
Buy food at a **buka** to recover Hunger, take a short rest, complete a single transport delivery run.

### 30-Minute Loop (Session Goals)
Complete a work shift, run an errand, visit a friend, manage a shop, handle one consequence.

### Daily / Life Loop (Days → Months)
Manage needs, earn income, spend, maintain relationships, protect assets, grow reputation, build a business, leave a mark.

---

## 3. Micro Loop (Inside a Single Session)

1. **Enter / Resume** — load into the persistent world at your last location or home.
2. **Assess State** — needs, money, messages, business notifications, wanted level.
3. **Set Intention** — *"I need money." "I need to sleep." "I want to meet someone." "I need to check my shop."*
4. **Move Through the World** — walk, run, drive, ride a danfo.
5. **Act** — work a shift, trade, talk, buy, sell, deliver, socialise, manage property.
6. **Receive Feedback** — money, needs, reputation, relationships, consequences.
7. **Decide Next Action or Log Off** — continue, or exit safely (preferably from housing).

---

## 4. Daily / Short-Term Loop

- Manage **needs** (hunger, energy, hygiene, bladder, fun, social)
- Generate **income** (job, business, trade)
- **Spend** on survival and improvement
- Maintain or grow **social connections**
- Protect or improve **housing and assets**

---

## 5. Medium-Term Loop (Days → Weeks)

- Stabilise housing and income
- Improve **skills** for your chosen path
- Build **relationships and reputation** in specific circles
- Acquire your **first vehicle** or better housing
- Begin **small economic activity** (side business, trading)

---

## 6. Long-Term Loop (Weeks → Months+)

- Own **significant property** or a business that employs/serves other players
- Achieve **recognised reputation** in the city
- Form or join **lasting social groups / crews / communities**
- Leave a **visible, persistent mark** on the economy and social fabric
- Optionally engage **higher-stakes systems** (bigger business, fictional crime, civic influence)

---

## 7. Loop Health Rules

- Every loop level must be **possible and interesting on mobile** in reasonable sessions.
- Loops must **create reasons to interact with other players**.
- Loops must **never require 24/7 attention** — persistence works in the player's favour when offline, within reason.
- **Failure opens new paths**, never pure dead ends.
