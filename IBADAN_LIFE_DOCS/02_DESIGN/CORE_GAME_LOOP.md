# CORE GAME LOOP – IBADAN LIFE

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active (Tier 1)  
**Depends On:** MASTER_GAME_DESIGN_DOCUMENT.md, DESIGN_PILLARS.md  

---

## 1. Philosophy

The core loop must continuously reinforce:

- Physical presence in the city  
- Interaction with other real players  
- Economic and social interdependence  
- Believable daily and long-term progression  

It must never collapse into pure menu management or isolated single-player activities.

---

## 2. Micro Loop (Inside a Single Session)

1. **Enter / Resume** – Load into the persistent world at last location or home.
2. **Assess State** – Needs, money, messages, business notifications, wanted level, etc.
3. **Set Immediate Intention** – “I need money”, “I need to sleep”, “I want to meet someone”, “I need to check my shop”.
4. **Move Through the World** – Walk, run, drive, take public transport, or use other movement.
5. **Act** – Work a shift, trade, talk, buy, sell, deliver, commit a fictional crime, socialise, manage property, etc.
6. **Receive Feedback** – Money changes, needs change, reputation changes, relationship changes, consequences.
7. **Decide Next Action or Log Off** – Continue the loop or safely exit (preferably from housing or a safe location).

---

## 3. Daily / Short-Term Loop

- Manage needs (hunger, energy, hygiene, mood, etc.)
- Generate income (job, business, trade, other activities)
- Spend on survival and improvement
- Maintain or grow social connections
- Protect or improve housing and assets

---

## 4. Medium-Term Loop (Days to Weeks)

- Stabilise housing and income
- Improve skills relevant to chosen path
- Build relationships and reputation in specific circles
- Acquire first vehicle or better housing
- Begin participating in or creating small economic activity (side business, trading, etc.)

---

## 5. Long-Term Loop (Weeks to Months+)

- Own significant property or a real business that employs or serves other players
- Achieve recognised reputation in the city
- Form or join lasting social groups / crews / communities
- Leave a visible, persistent mark on the player-driven economy and social fabric
- Optional: engage deeply with riskier systems (higher-stakes business, fictional crime, politics-like reputation systems if added later)

---

## 6. Loop Health Rules

- Every loop level must remain possible and interesting on mobile in reasonable session lengths.
- Loops must create reasons to interact with other players.
- Loops must never require constant 24/7 attention (persistence should work in the player’s favour when offline, within reason).
- Failure in a loop should open new interesting paths rather than pure dead ends.
