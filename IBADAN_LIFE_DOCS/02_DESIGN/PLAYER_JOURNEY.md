> **North Star:** the story of a player from stranger to citizen — the beats every player travels, at their own pace.

---

## 1. The Journey in One Line

**Arrive with nothing → find your footing → earn your place → build your name → become part of the city.**

---

## 2. Stage 1 — Arrival (First Session)

**State:** New. Penniless (or in debt, if Lapo). No home. No one knows you.

**Experience:**
- Create your character; choose your background.
- Land in the **UI Student Hub**.
- Learn to move; take in the city.
- First meal, first coins, first conversation.

**Goal given:** *"Find a place to sleep and a way to earn."*

**Exit when:** the player has shelter (even a hostel bed) and one income source.

---

## 3. Stage 2 — Footing (First Days)

**State:** A routine forms. A few coins. A couple of contacts.

**Experience:**
- Work shifts; small purchases; short rides (danfo, okada).
- Meet neighbours; join a chat; get recognised at a buka.
- First setback: a need neglected, a fine, a debt.

**Goal given:** *"Stabilise — steady income, steady sleep."*

**Exit when:** stable housing + regular income.

---

## 4. Stage 3 — Earning Your Place (First Weeks)

**State:** Skills grow. Relationships deepen. Assets appear.

**Experience:**
- Better job or first side hustle.
- First vehicle (keke, okada, or a used micra).
- Reputation in one district — people know your name.
- First real choice: invest, save, or spend.

**Goal given:** *"Own something — a ride, a room, a small shop."*

**Exit when:** the player owns an asset.

---

## 5. Stage 4 — Building Your Name (Months)

**State:** Established. Known. Invested in the city.

**Experience:**
- Run a business that employs/serves other players.
- Build a circle — friends, crew, community.
- Shape your district: your shop, your block, your reputation.
- Face bigger stakes: debt, crime, competition, legacy.

**Goal given:** *"Leave a mark the city remembers."*

**Exit when:** the player has a lasting, visible mark.

---

## 6. Journey Map (Beats × Feelings)

| Beat | Arrival | Footing | Earning a Place | Building a Name |
|------|---------|---------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Core action** | Move, look, eat | Work, earn, rest | Skill, buy, travel | Build, hire, lead |
| **Key emotion** | Wonder | Competence | Pride | Belonging |
| **Social** | Strangers | Acquaintances | Friends | Community |
| **Economy** | First coins | Wages | Assets | Business |
| **Risk** | None | Small setbacks | Debt, fines | Crime, competition |

---

## 7. Journey Rules

- **Every player travels the same beats, at their own pace.** No one is forced; no one is rushed.
- **Shortcuts exist but cost.** A Nepo starts ahead; a Lapo starts behind but the hustle is the story.
- **The city is the guide.** Goals emerge from the world, not a quest log.
- **Returning players are welcomed back** — the city kept their place.
