---

## Purpose

Defines exactly what is in the **MVP** and what is explicitly deferred.

---

## MVP Must-Have Systems

### World
- One fully playable, high-quality district (or tightly connected set) that feels like real Ibadan
- Functional **day/night cycle**
- Basic **world streaming**
- Enterable key buildings (housing, workplaces, shops, social spaces)

### Player
- Full **character creation** (background + avatar)
- **Third-person movement** (walk, run, interact)
- Basic **needs** system
- Simple **skill/attribute** foundation
- Persistent character data

### Economy & Survival
- **Currency (Naira, ₦)**
- Several **job types** real players can perform
- Buy **food, basic items**, pay for **housing**
- Simple **player-to-player trading**

### Housing
- **Rent** or obtain basic housing
- Housing as a **safe logout** point and needs-recovery location

### Social
- **Proximity presence** of other players
- Basic **text communication**
- Simple **friend / relationship** foundation
- Other real players visible and interactable as the **primary population**

### Multiplayer Foundation
- Persistent character and world state
- **Server authority** on critical data (money, inventory, ownership, position)
- Basic session management and reconnection

### Onboarding
- A clear **first-hour experience**: character → city → shelter path → first income path

---

## Explicitly Out of MVP (Deferred)

- Full multi-district city
- Deep business ownership/management (a basic version may exist)
- Complex romance / family systems
- Advanced crime and wanted systems (a basic version possible)
- Vehicles beyond a very limited set
- Advanced customisation and fashion
- Large-scale events and live ops
- Voice chat
- Complex politics / city management
- Full public transport network
- High-fidelity interiors for every building

---

## MVP Success Criteria

- A new player reaches *"I have a place to sleep and a way to make money"* within the first session or two.
- **Real players** are clearly visible and interactable as the main population.
- The district feels like a **place**, not a collection of maps.
- **Performance** is acceptable on target mid-range Android devices.
- The **core loop is fun and meaningful** without the later systems.
