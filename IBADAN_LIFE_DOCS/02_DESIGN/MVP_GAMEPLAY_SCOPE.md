**Status:** Active (Tier 1)

## Purpose

This document defines exactly what must be present in the first publicly releasable version (MVP) and what is explicitly deferred.

The MVP must already deliver the core fantasy:  
a player can create a character, enter a 3D city, move freely, find shelter, generate income, interact with other real players, and begin building a life.

---

## MVP Must-Have Systems

### World
- One fully playable, high-quality district (or tightly connected set of areas) that feels like a real part of Ibadan
- Functional day/night cycle
- Basic world streaming
- Enterable key buildings (housing, workplaces, shops, social spaces)

### Player
- Full character creation
- Third-person movement (walk, run, basic interaction)
- Basic needs system
- Simple skill/attribute foundation
- Persistent character data

### Economy & Survival
- Currency
- Basic income sources (at least several job types that real players can perform)
- Ability to buy food, basic items, and pay for housing
- Simple player-to-player trading

### Housing
- Ability to rent or otherwise obtain basic housing
- Housing as a safe logout point and needs recovery location

### Social
- Proximity-based presence of other players
- Basic text communication
- Simple friend / relationship foundation
- Ability to see and interact with other real players as the primary population

### Multiplayer Foundation
- Persistent character and world state
- Server authority on critical data (money, inventory, ownership, position)
- Basic session management and reconnection

### Onboarding
- Clear first-hour experience that gets the player into the city with a character, shelter path, and first income path

---

## Explicitly Out of MVP (Deferred)

- Full multi-district city
- Deep business ownership and management (basic version may exist, full simulation later)
- Complex romance / family systems
- Advanced crime and wanted systems (basic version possible)
- Vehicles beyond very limited set
- Advanced customization and fashion systems
- Large-scale events and live ops
- Voice chat
- Complex politics or city management systems
- Full public transport network
- High-fidelity interiors for every building

---

## MVP Success Criteria

- A new player can reach “I have a place to sleep and a way to make money” within the first session or two.
- Real players are clearly visible and interactable as the main population.
- The district feels like a place, not a collection of maps.
- Performance is acceptable on target mid-range Android devices.
- The core loop is already fun and meaningful without the later systems.
