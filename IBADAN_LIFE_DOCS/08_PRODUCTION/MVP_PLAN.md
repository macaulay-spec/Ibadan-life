---

## 1. MVP Definition

The MVP is the first version a player can **create a character, enter a 3D city, move freely, find shelter, earn income, interact with real players, and begin building a life** — and want to return.

(Full scope: MVP_GAMEPLAY_SCOPE.md)

---

## 2. MVP 1 — Proof of Concept

**Map:** UI Student Hub & Samonda Corridor  
**Density:** 32 players/instance

**Careers:** okada driver, buka staff, vendor, mechanic, bank teller, traffic police  
**Vehicles:** keke, micra  
**Systems:** basic needs, local chat, housing rent

**Goal:** prove presence, movement, a survival loop, and real-player interaction in one district.

---

## 3. MVP 2 — Expansion

**Map:** + Dugbe CBD & Iwo Road  
**Density:** 64 players/instance

**Careers:** + logistics operator, real estate agent  
**Vehicles:** + danfo, cargo vans  
**Systems:** + full supply chain, player shops, district governance

**Goal:** prove the **player-driven economy** and a multi-district city.

---

## 4. Path to MVP

1. **Vertical Slice** (UI Student Hub) — prove the fantasy.
2. **Harden** the stack: persistence, replication, performance, security.
3. **MVP 1** — one district, real loop, real players.
4. **MVP 2** — expand districts, deepen economy and social systems.
5. **Live** — soft launch, iterate on retention and economy health.

---

## 5. MVP Success Criteria

- A new player reaches **"a place to sleep + a way to earn"** within the first session or two.
- **Real players** are clearly the main population.
- The district feels like a **place**, not a map collection.
- **Performance** acceptable on target mid-range Android.
- The **core loop is fun** without the later systems.

---

## 6. What MVP Is Not

MVP is **not** a full city, deep business sim, or polished everything. It is the **smallest thing that proves the fantasy** — and it must already feel like IBADAN LIFE.
