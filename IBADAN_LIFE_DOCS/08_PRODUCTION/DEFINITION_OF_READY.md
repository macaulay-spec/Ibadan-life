> **North Star:** a feature starts only when it is truly ready — clear, scoped, pillar-aligned, and unblocked. No starting work we cannot finish well.

---

## A feature is READY to build when ALL of these are true:

### Clarity
- [ ] The **fantasy** it serves is stated in one sentence.
- [ ] Its **scope** is written (in scope / out of scope).
- [ ] Its **acceptance criteria** are concrete and testable.

### Alignment
- [ ] It serves **at least one design pillar** and undermines none.
- [ ] It violates **no Non-Goal**.
- [ ] Its **documentation** exists and is consistent with the Source of Truth.

### Feasibility
- [ ] Its **dependencies** (FEATURE_DEPENDENCY_MAP.md) are **green**.
- [ ] It fits the current **milestone** (MILESTONE_PLAN.md).
- [ ] The **performance budget** impact is estimated and acceptable.

### Ownership
- [ ] It has a named **owner** (human or agent role).
- [ ] It has a **Definition of Done** checklist (DEFINITION_OF_DONE.md).

### Safety
- [ ] Its **moderation / safety** implications are identified.
- [ ] Its **analytics events** are defined.

---

## Rule

**If a box is unchecked, the feature is not ready.** Do not start it. Clarify, align, or build the dependency first. Starting unready work is how projects die.
