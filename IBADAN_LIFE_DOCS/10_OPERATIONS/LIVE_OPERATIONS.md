## 1. Philosophy

IBADAN LIFE is a **live service**. The city does not stop when we ship — it **grows**. Live Ops keeps it healthy: events that bring people together, updates that fix and improve, and a watchful eye on the economy and the community.

## 3. Events & Content

- **In-world events:** market days, owambe celebrations, campus events, district festivals.
- **Seasonal content:** fashion seasons, furniture themes, limited-time items.
- **World changes:** new shops, shifting district character, player-driven landmarks.
- Events **support** the living city; they never replace **player agency**.

---

## 4. Cadence

- **Daily:** health checks (crashes, economy, moderation queue).
- **Weekly:** economy review, community pulse, content planning.
- **Monthly/Seasonal:** roadmap updates, bigger events, feature releases.

## 5. The Live Ops Promise

> *The city is alive. We keep it that way — fairly, safely, and with pride.*
