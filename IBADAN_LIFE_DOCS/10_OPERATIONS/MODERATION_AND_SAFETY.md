> **North Star:** a city where players feel safe — because a player-driven social economy is only worth having if people trust it.

---

## 1. Why Safety Is Core

Our economy and society are **built on real players trusting each other**. Harassment, fraud, exploitation, and abuse break that trust. **Trust and safety are features, designed from day one** — not a post-launch patch.

---

## 2. Safety Pillars

| Pillar | Practice |
|--------|----------|
| **Prevention** | Design that reduces harm (proximity limits, safe spaces, defaults) |
| **Protection** | Reporting, blocking, muting — always available, one tap |
| **Moderation** | Human + automated review of reports and behaviour |
| **Consequences** | Graduated, fair enforcement (warn → mute → suspend → ban) |
| **Support** | Player support and account recovery |
| **Privacy** | Data handled with care (PRIVACY_AND_DATA_PROTECTION.md) |

---

## 3. Player Tools (Day One)

- **Report** any player, chat, business, or content — with reason categories.
- **Block / mute** — immediate, reversible, private.
- **Safe spaces** — your home is protected; you control who enters.
- **Privacy controls** — who can message you, see your status, find you.

---

## 4. Enforcement (Graduated & Fair)

| Level | Action |
|-------|--------|
| **Warn** | Education; a chance to correct |
| **Mute / restrict** | Temporary limits on chat or features |
| **Suspend** | Temporary removal from the city |
| **Ban** | Permanent removal for serious or repeated harm |

- **Appeals** are always possible.
- **Transparency:** players know the rules (CONTENT_AND_COMMUNITY_POLICY.md).

---

## 5. Automated + Human

- **Automated detection** for spam, fraud patterns, and known-bad content.
- **Human review** for nuanced cases and appeals.
- **Logging** for audit and economy reconciliation.

---

## 6. Special Protections

- **Minors and vulnerable players** get extra protection and reporting priority.
- **Real-money risk** (scams, fraud) is treated as a safety issue, not just an economy issue.

---

## 7. Rules

1. **Safety is a Definition-of-Done gate** for any social or economic feature.
2. **Report, block, mute** are always one tap away.
3. **Enforcement is fair, graduated, and appealable.**
4. **We protect the player's data** as we protect their experience.
