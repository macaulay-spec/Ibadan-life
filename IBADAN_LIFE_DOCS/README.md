# IBADAN LIFE

### *Your city. Your hustle. Your story.*

**Version:** 2.0  
**Phase:** Pre-Production → Vertical Slice  
**Engine:** Unreal Engine 5 · Mobile-first (mid-range Android)  
**Status:** Active — one of the best games in the world, in the making

---

IBADAN LIFE is a **persistent, 3D, open-world, multiplayer life simulation** set in a city inspired by **Ibadan, Nigeria**. Real players are the population. They walk the streets, work real jobs, run real businesses, own property, make friends and enemies, and build lives that continue even when they log off.

> The city is not a backdrop. It is a society.

## ⚖️ The Rules That Govern Everything

1. **Tiers rule.** A lower-tier document never contradicts a higher-tier one. ([SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md](00_MASTER/SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md))
2. **Pillars rule.** Every feature serves at least one of the six design pillars and undermines none. ([DESIGN_PILLARS.md](01_PRODUCT/DESIGN_PILLARS.md))
3. **Brand rule.** Specific, confident, warm, respectful, zero filler. ([BRAND_AND_TONE_OF_VOICE.md](00_MASTER/BRAND_AND_TONE_OF_VOICE.md))
4. **Decisions rule.** Major choices → `ADR/`. Agent choices → `AGENT_DECISIONS/`.

## 🏙️ The City at a Glance

Six districts, each with its own soul:

| District | Character |
|----------|-----------|
| **UI Student Hub** | Academic, high-density, low-cost — hostels, tech kiosks, hustle |
| **Dugbe CBD** | Commercial, corporate, finance — Cocoa House, banking plaza |
| **Bodija Central** | Upscale residential & market — gated estates, nightlife |
| **Iwo Transit Hub** | Transport & wholesale — bus park, mechanic village |
| **Bower's Historic** | Heritage, low-income — the Tower, rust-roof neighbourhoods |
| **Agodi Green Zone** | Administrative & recreational — secretariat, lake, golf |
