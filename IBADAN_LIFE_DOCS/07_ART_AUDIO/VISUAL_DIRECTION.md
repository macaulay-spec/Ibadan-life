## 1. The Look in One Line

**A warm, sunlit, stylised Ibadan — rust-tinted roofs, red earth, busy streets — coherent, alive, and performant on mobile.**

---

## 2. Direction Principles

| Principle | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **Stylised, not realistic** | A coherent art direction over raw fidelity |
| **Warm & sunlit** | Big skies, golden light, glowing nights |
| **Specific to Ibadan** | Landmarks, materials, and rhythms of a real city |
| **Human-centred** | People and their streets are the heroes |
| **Mobile-smart** | Every choice respects the performance budget |

---

## 3. Palette

- **Earth:** red-laterite soil, warm browns, dust.
- **Sky:** big, warm daylight; golden dusk; deep, glowing night.
- **City:** rust-tinted corrugated roofs; bright signage; hand-painted shopfronts.
- **People:** rich, diverse Nigerian skin tones and attire.
- **Accent:** market and signage colour — vibrant, purposeful.

---

## 4. Light

- **Day:** warm, directional sunlight; long shadows.
- **Dusk:** golden hour; the city wakes its lights.
- **Night:** streetlights, shop signs, vehicle headlights — the city glows.
- **Baked lighting** (GPU Lightmass) delivers quality at low runtime cost.

---

---

## 6. What We Avoid

- ❌ Photorealism (a non-goal).
- ❌ Generic "African village" tropes.
- ❌ Visual noise that hurts readability or FPS.
- ❌ Cultural caricature.
