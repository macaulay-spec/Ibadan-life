> **North Star:** a city you can feel — the rust-tinted roofs, the red earth, the busy streets, the people — rendered with a coherent, mobile-smart art direction.

---

## 1. Art Philosophy

Art serves the fantasy: **you are really in Ibadan.** Every asset must feel **specific, warm, and alive**, and run on a **mid-range Android phone**. We choose **stylised coherence over raw fidelity** — a look that is unmistakably ours and performant everywhere.

---

## 2. The Ibadan Look (Visual Identity)

- **Rust-tinted corrugated roofs** — the city's signature.
- **Red-laterite earth**, unpaved edges, dust.
- **A university on a hill**; academic corridors.
- **Dense commercial hubs** (Dugbe), **sprawling markets** (Bodija), **heritage monuments** (Bower's Tower), **green administrative calm** (Agodi).
- **Bright signage, hand-painted shopfronts, busy streets.**
- **Vehicles:** yellow danfo, okada, keke, micra, private cars.

---

## 3. Colour & Light

- **Warm, sunlit days** — big skies, long shadows.
- **Golden dusk**; **glowing nights** — streetlights, shop signs, headlights.
- A **warm, earthy palette** with pops of market and signage colour.
- **Baked lighting** (GPU Lightmass) for quality at low runtime cost.

---

## 4. Characters

- **Modular avatars** (see CHARACTER_ART_BIBLE.md): shared rig, merged parts, LOD'd.
- **Expressive faces** (morph targets) so players recognise each other.
- **Diverse, authentic Nigerian appearance** — skin tones, features, attire.
- **Attire as identity:** traditional (agbada, iro and buba, gele, fila, aso-oke, Ankara) and contemporary (streetwear, varsity, corporate, mechanic overalls, transport uniforms).

---

## 5. Environments

- **Districts with distinct character** (see ENVIRONMENT_ART_BIBLE.md and DISTRICT_BIBLE.md).
- **Enterable interiors** (bukas, homes, banks, shops) — functional and consistent.
- Detail concentrates where players gather; edges are lighter (performance).

---

## 6. Art Principles

1. **Authentic over generic.** Specific Ibadan, not "African-themed."
2. **Coherent over flashy.** One strong, consistent look.
3. **Performant over pretty.** If it tanks FPS, it goes.
4. **Human-centred.** People and their streets are the heroes.
5. **Respectful.** We represent a real culture with care (see CULTURAL_AUTHENTICITY_GUIDE.md).
