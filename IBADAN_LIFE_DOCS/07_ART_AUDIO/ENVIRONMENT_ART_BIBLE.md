> **North Star:** streets, markets, homes, and landmarks that feel like Ibadan — built to stream beautifully on a mid-range phone.

---

## 1. The World We Build

A **compressed, stylised Ibadan**: the feeling and landmarks of a real city, not a 1:1 map. Six districts, each with a **distinct, recognisable character**, connected by roads and transport.

---

## 2. Environment Principles

| Principle | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **District identity** | Each district recognisable in 30 seconds |
| **Landmark-driven** | Players navigate by landmarks, not coordinates |
| **Enterable** | Key buildings have functional interiors |
| **Alive** | The world feels busy, used, and real |
| **Streamable** | World Partition + LOD + baked lighting for mobile |

---

## 3. Key Environment Types

| Type | Examples | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| **Streets & roads** | Arterials, neighbourhood streets, alleys | The connective tissue; traffic and flow |
| **Markets** | Bodija Market, roadside markets | Dense, loud, alive; the economic heart |
| **Housing** | Hostels, flats, compounds, estates | Enterable; reflects district character |
| **Workplaces** | Bukas, banks, offices, mechanic village | Where work happens |
| **Social spaces** | Parks, venues, nightlife, campus | Where life happens |
| **Landmarks** | UI Main Gate, Cocoa House, Bower's Tower, Agodi Lake | Orientation and pride |
| **Interiors** | Homes, shops, bukas, banks | Functional, consistent, lightweight |

---

## 5. Materials & Identity

- **Rust-tinted corrugated metal** — the signature roof.
- **Red-laterite earth**, concrete, painted plaster.
- **Hand-painted signage**, bright market colour.
- Real, tactile materials — the city feels **lived-in**.

---

## 6. Performance

- **World Partition** tiles stream by proximity.
- **LODs** on all major props.
- **Baked lighting** (GPU Lightmass) for quality at low cost.
- Detail concentrates where players gather; edges are lighter.

---

## 7. Authenticity

Every environment passes the **Respect Checklist**. If it does not feel like **Ibadan**, it does not ship.
