> **North Star:** a city that feels like home to the people who live in it — Ibadan is a real place with a real soul, and we earn the right to represent it.

---

## 1. Why Authenticity Is a Design Requirement

Players will **know** if we fake it. A generic "African city" or a Lagos copy fails the people we are building for. **Authentic Ibadan texture** is a design pillar — and a retention feature. We do not borrow Ibadan's name; **we earn it.**

---

## 2. The Authenticity Mandate

Every world, character, sound, and string must pass this check:

> **Would someone who knows Ibadan recognise the soul of the place — and feel respected?**

---

## 3. Specificity Over Stereotype

| Generic (rejected) | Specific (required) |
|--------------------|---------------------|
| "African village" | Rust-tinted corrugated roofs; red-laterite earth; a university on a hill |
| "Local food" | Amala and abula from a buka; suya at dusk |
| "Transport" | Danfo, okada, keke, micra on real routes |
| "Traditional clothes" | Agbada, iro and buba, gele, fila, aso-oke, Ankara |
| "A market" | Bodija Market — sprawling, loud, alive |
| "Music" | Afrobeats, fuji, street sounds, the call to prayer, danfo horns |

---

## 4. Language & Voice

- Default: clear international English.
- **Yorùbá and Pidgin** used respectfully and **always defined on first use** (see PROJECT_GLOSSARY.md).
- **No caricature.** Slang is used with care. When in doubt: *would a Nigerian player feel respected?*
- Names, shop signs, and dialogue should feel **real** — not costume.

---

## 5. Cultural Elements We Represent (With Care)

| Element | How we honour it |
|---------|------------------|
| **Landmarks** | UI, Cocoa House, Bower's Tower, Bodija, Agodi, Dugbe — stylised, recognisable |
| **Food** | Buka culture, amala, suya, market provisions |
| **Transport** | Danfo, okada, keke, micra — real routes and behaviour |
| **Dress** | Agbada, iro/buba, gele, fila, aso-oke, Ankara, streetwear, uniforms |
| **Social life** | Hustle, owambe, market banter, campus life, neighbourhoods |
| **Rhythm** | Day/night, market days, dusk suya, nightlife |
| **Values** | Hustle, respect, community, family, pride, resilience |

---

## 6. The Respect Checklist (Every Asset)

Before any cultural asset ships, ask:

1. Is it **specific** (Ibadan/Nigerian), not generic?
2. Is it **respectful** — no stereotype, no mockery?
3. Is local language **defined** for global players?
4. Would a **Nigerian reviewer** approve?
5. Does it serve the **fantasy** (presence, pride, belonging)?

---

## 7. Research Foundation

Grounded in the **Ibadan Life Research Brief** (22-page pre-production bible): geography, culture, transport, economy, and social dynamics of contemporary Ibadan and urban Nigeria.

---

## 8. Rule

**Authenticity is non-negotiable.** An asset that fails the Respect Checklist does not ship, regardless of beauty or schedule.
