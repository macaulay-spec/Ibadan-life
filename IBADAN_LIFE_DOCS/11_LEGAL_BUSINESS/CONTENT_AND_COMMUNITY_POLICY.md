**Version:** 1.0

> **North Star:** a city with clear, fair rules — so every player knows what is welcome, what is not, and that the rules are applied consistently.

---

## 1. Our Commitment

IBADAN LIFE is a **shared city built on real players**. That only works when everyone knows the rules and trusts they are applied fairly. This policy is public, plain, and consistently enforced.

---

## 2. Our Values

- **Respect.** Treat every player as a person. No harassment, hate, or abuse.
- **Authenticity.** Be real. No impersonation, fraud, or deception.
- **Safety.** Protect the vulnerable. No exploitation, especially of minors.
- **Fairness.** Play the game, don't cheat the game or its players.
- **Pride.** Represent your community and your city with dignity.

---

## 3. Allowed

- Hustle, ambition, competition, rivalry — **in the game**.
- Self-expression through avatar, shop, crew, and content.
- Humour, banter, and culture — used respectfully.
- Disagreement and debate — civil and constructive.

---

## 4. Not Allowed

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **Harassment & hate** | Abuse, threats, slurs, targeted harassment, hate speech |
| **Exploitation** | Scams, fraud, grooming, exploiting minors or vulnerable players |
| **Cheating** | Hacking, botting, exploiting, real-money trading of assets |
| **Impersonation** | Pretending to be a player, brand, or staff member |
| **Sexual content / nudity** | Not permitted; the city is for a broad audience |
| **Real-world harm** | Doxxing, swatting, inciting real-world harm |
| **Illegal real-world activity** | No real criminal instruction or coordination |

---

## 5. Enforcement

Enforcement is **graduated, fair, and appealable** (see MODERATION_AND_SAFETY.md):

**Warn → Mute/Restrict → Suspend → Ban**

- **Appeals** are always possible.
- **Serious harm** (exploitation, threats, fraud) can skip to **immediate suspension or ban**.

---

## 6. Player Responsibility

- **Report** what breaks these rules (one tap).
- **Block/mute** anyone you do not want to hear.
- **Protect your account** — we will never ask for your password.

---

## 7. Our Promise

> *A fair city, fairly run. Everyone knows the rules. Everyone is heard. Everyone is safe.*
