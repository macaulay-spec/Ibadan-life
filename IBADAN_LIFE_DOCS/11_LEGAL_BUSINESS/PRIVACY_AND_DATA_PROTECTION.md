> **North Star:** we earn players' trust with their data — collect little, protect it fiercely, and give players control over their own information.

---

## 1. Our Commitment

Players trust us with their identity, their money, and their social lives. We honour that: **privacy by design**, **data minimisation**, and **player control** — and compliance with applicable data-protection laws (including Nigeria's **NDPA 2023** and, where relevant, GDPR for international players).

---

## 2. Principles

| Principle | Practice |
|-----------|----------|
| **Minimisation** | Collect only what the game genuinely needs |
| **Purpose limitation** | Data is used only for the purpose it was collected for |
| **Security** | Encryption in transit and at rest; least-privilege access |
| **Transparency** | Players know what we collect and why |
| **Control** | Players can access, correct, export, and delete their data |
| **Retention** | Data is kept only as long as needed, then deleted |

---

## 3. What We Collect (And Why)

| Data | Why |
|------|-----|
| **Account credentials** | Identity and account security |
| **Player & progress data** | The persistent life (character, wealth, items, relationships) |
| **Economy ledger** | Fair, auditable transactions; fraud prevention |
| **Device/anti-fraud signals** | Account protection and security |
| **Analytics (aggregated)** | Improving the game (privacy-respecting) |
| **Moderation records** | Safety and enforcement |
| **Support communications** | Helping players |

We do **not** collect data we do not need, and we do **not** sell personal data.

---

## 4. Player Rights

Players can:
- **Access** their data.
- **Correct** inaccuracies.
- **Export** their data.
- **Delete** their account and data (**soft deletion** supported — the city remembers, but you can leave).
- **Object / restrict** certain processing, where law provides.

---

## 5. Security & Retention

- **Encryption** in transit (TLS) and at rest for sensitive data.
- **Least-privilege** access; audited.
- **Retention schedules** per data type; secure deletion after.
- **Breach response** follows INCIDENT_AND_HOTFIX_PROCESS.md, with player notification where required.

---

## 6. Children & Vulnerable Players

- Extra protection for **minors**; age-appropriate data handling (see AGE_RATING_AND_LEGAL.md).
- **Never** exploit or profile vulnerable players.

---

## 7. Rule

**Handle every player's data as if it were your own.** If we would not want it done to us, we do not do it to them.
