> **North Star:** a sustainable, ethical business — we earn from cosmetics, convenience, and native advertising, never from pay-to-win or player exploitation.

---

## 1. Core Rule: No Pay-to-Win

**Pay-to-win mechanics are strictly prohibited.** Monetisation must never destroy the **fairness of the player-driven economy** or progression. Money buys **cosmetics and convenience** — never power.

---

## 2. Revenue Streams (Ethical Framework)

| Category | Model | What it is | Economic impact |
|----------|-------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Cosmetics & Fashion** | Direct purchase | Native outfits (aso-oke), vehicle wraps, hairstyles | Purely visual; **zero stat boosts** |
| **Furniture / Season Pass** | Seasonal pass | Interior decor themes (Modern Minimalist, Traditional Royal Court) | Decorative blueprints; stats match base equivalents |
| **In-Game Billboard Ad Network** | B2B | Real-world businesses buy native billboard/plot space via an ad portal | Real corporate revenue; **no cost to players** |
| **Business Storefront Customisation** | Direct purchase | Neon signage, unique shop façades | Increases visibility; no price/stat changes |

---

## 3. What We Never Sell

- ❌ Currency (Naira) for advantage — no buying power.
- ❌ Stat boosts, XP boosts, or progression skips that create advantage.
- ❌ Loot boxes that gate power or exploit gambling psychology.
- ❌ Anything that lets a paying player dominate a non-paying player.

---

## 4. Design Principles

1. **Fairness first.** The Lapo and the whale can stand in the same street as equals.
2. **Cosmetic, not power.** If it changes how you look, it is monetisable. If it changes what you can do, it is not.
3. **Native, not interruptive.** Advertising lives **in the world** (billboards, plots), not in the player's face.
4. **Transparent.** Players always know what they are buying and what it does.

---

## 5. The In-World Ad Network

- Real-world businesses buy **native billboard space** through an ad portal.
- Billboards are **physical objects in the city** — part of the streetscape, not pop-ups.
- Generates real corporate revenue **without imposing costs on players**.

---

## 6. Sustainability

The model is built to sustain a **multi-year live service**: cosmetics and seasonal content for players, native advertising for brands, and a healthy internal economy that keeps the city alive.

---

## 7. Rule

**If a monetisation idea makes a paying player strictly more powerful than a non-paying player, it is rejected.** Full stop.
