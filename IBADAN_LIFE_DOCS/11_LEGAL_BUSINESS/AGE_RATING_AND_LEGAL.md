---

## 1. Target Rating

IBADAN LIFE is designed for a **teen-and-up** audience (roughly **13+ / PEGI 12–16 range**), subject to **formal classification** in each launch market. Final ratings are obtained from the relevant bodies (e.g., **PEGI, ESRB, Google Play, Apple App Store**) before release in each territory.

> Ratings are **earned by content**, confirmed by the classification bodies — not self-declared.

---

## 2. Content That Shapes the Rating

| Content | How we handle it |
|---------|------------------|
| **Fictional crime & violence** | Stylised, consequence-driven; **no graphic violence**; no real-world criminal instruction |
| **Economy & gambling-like mechanics** | **No loot boxes, no gambling**; cosmetics only; no pay-to-win |
| **Social & chat** | **Moderated**; reporting/blocking from day one |
| **Romance / relationships** | wholesome, consensual, non-explicit |
| **Language** | Profanity filtered; hate speech prohibited and enforced |
| **Substances / real-world references** | No promotion of real drugs, weapons, or illegal activity |

---

## 3. Protecting Minors

- **Age-appropriate** defaults and data handling (PRIVACY_AND_DATA_PROTECTION.md).
- **No targeted advertising** to minors.
- **No exploitative mechanics** aimed at young or vulnerable players.
- **Parental awareness** — clear, honest store listings and content descriptions.

---

## 4. Legal Compliance

- **Data protection:** NDPA 2023 (Nigeria), GDPR (international), and local laws per market.
- **Consumer protection:** honest monetisation; clear pricing; no dark patterns.
- **Platform policies:** Google Play and Apple App Store rules for games, IAP, ads, and user-generated content.
- **Advertising:** the in-world ad network complies with ad regulations; no deceptive ads.
- **IP & cultural respect:** we represent a real culture with care; no misappropriation.

---

## 5. User-Generated Content

Players create names, shops, signs, and chat. We manage this with:

- **Proactive filters** (profanity, slurs, hate).
- **Reactive moderation** (reports, enforcement).
- **Clear policy** (CONTENT_AND_COMMUNITY_POLICY.md).

---
