---

## 1. World Philosophy

The city is a **continuous, physically explorable 3D space** inspired by Ibadan. It is not a collection of loading zones or menu locations. It must feel like a **real place** — geography, districts of different character, landmarks, transport arteries, and living rhythms.

**Real players are the primary population.** The world simulation *supports and amplifies* player activity; it never replaces it.

---

## 2. Inspirational Sources

- Real geography and districts of Ibadan — hills, **rust-tinted corrugated roofs**, red-laterite earth, the **University of Ibadan**, Bodija, Dugbe, Mapo, Agodi, Challenge, Iwo Road.
- Contemporary Nigerian urban life: humour, **transport culture** (danfo, okada, keke, micra), **market culture**, food (buka, amala), and social norms.
- The feeling of a city that is simultaneously **academic, commercial, residential, and historically layered**.

Exact 1:1 recreation of real streets is **not required**. **Emotional and cultural authenticity is required.**

---

## 4. District Philosophy

Each district has:
- A **distinct visual and economic character**
- **Key landmarks** and gathering points
- Different **housing and business** opportunities
- A different **social flavour**

Districts connect by **roads, paths, and transport** so that movement between them is a meaningful part of play.

---

## 5. Key World Systems (Overview)

- **Time of day and calendar** (TIME_AND_DAY_NIGHT.md)
- Weather
- Ambient population support (NPCs for traffic, basic services, atmosphere)
- **Transport network** (danfo, okada, keke, micra, private vehicles)
- **Building enterability** and interior logic
- **World streaming** for mobile performance
- **Persistence** of player-created changes (businesses, property states)

---

## 6. Cultural Authenticity Mandate

All world design decisions must be checked against **CULTURAL_AUTHENTICITY_GUIDE.md**. The city must feel **specifically like an Ibadan-inspired Nigerian place** — not a generic environment with Nigerian flags applied.
