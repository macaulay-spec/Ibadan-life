# WORLD BIBLE – IBADAN LIFE

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active (Tier 1)  
**Depends On:** PRODUCT_VISION.md, DESIGN_PILLARS.md  

---

## 1. World Philosophy

The city is a continuous, physically explorable 3D space inspired by Ibadan.  
It is not a collection of loading zones or menu locations.  
It must feel like a real place with geography, districts of different character, landmarks, transport arteries, and living rhythms.

Real players are the primary population. The world simulation supports and amplifies player activity rather than replacing it.

---

## 2. Inspirational Sources

- Real geography and districts of Ibadan (hills, red rooftops, University of Ibadan, Bodija, Dugbe, Mapo Hall, Cocoa House, Agodi, Challenge, Ring Road, etc.)
- Contemporary Nigerian urban life, humour, transport culture, market culture, and social norms
- The feeling of a city that is simultaneously academic, commercial, residential, and historically layered

Exact 1:1 recreation of real streets is not required.  
Emotional and cultural authenticity is required.

---

## 3. District Philosophy

The city is divided into districts, each with:
- Distinct visual and economic character
- Key landmarks and gathering points
- Different housing and business opportunities
- Different social flavour

Districts are connected by roads, paths, and transport options so that movement between them is a meaningful part of play.

---

## 4. Key World Systems (Overview)

- Time of day and calendar
- Weather
- Ambient population support (NPCs for traffic, basic services, atmosphere)
- Transport network
- Building enterability and interior logic
- World streaming for mobile performance
- Persistence of player-created changes (businesses, property states, etc.)

Detailed specifications live in the companion documents in this folder.

---

## 5. Cultural Authenticity Mandate

All world design decisions must be checked against CULTURAL_AUTHENTICITY_GUIDE.md.  
The city must feel specifically like an Ibadan-inspired Nigerian place, not a generic environment with Nigerian flags applied.
