> **North Star:** six districts, six souls — the neighbourhoods that make the city feel like Ibadan.

---

## 1. Why Districts Matter

A player should be able to say *"I'm from Dugbe"* or *"I hustle at Bodija"* and mean something. Each district has its own **look, economy, housing, and social flavour**, connected by roads and transport so movement between them is part of the game.

---

## 2. The Six Districts

### UI Student Hub — *The Hustle Starts Here*
- **Character:** Academic, high-density, low-cost.
- **Landmarks:** UI Main Gate, student hostels, tech kiosks, printing shops, cheap bukas.
- **Economy:** Student jobs, tech services, budget food, hostel rent.
- **Housing:** Hostels, shared apartments, small rooms.
- **Social flavour:** Young, ambitious, loud, always connected.
- **Instance density:** 96 players.

### Dugbe CBD — *Where the Money Moves*
- **Character:** Commercial, corporate, finance.
- **Landmarks:** Cocoa House (compressed), Dugbe Mall, Banking Plaza.
- **Economy:** Corporate jobs, banking, retail, professional services.
- **Housing:** Apartments, serviced flats.
- **Social flavour:** Polished, fast, status-conscious.
- **Instance density:** 48 players.

### Bodija Central — *Live Large*
- **Character:** Upscale residential and market.
- **Landmarks:** Bodija Market, luxury nightclubs, gated estates.
- **Economy:** Market trade, nightlife, premium retail, estates.
- **Housing:** Gated estates, luxury apartments.
- **Social flavour:** Wealthy, visible, celebratory (owambe energy).
- **Instance density:** 64 players.

### Iwo Transit Hub — *The City Moves Here*
- **Character:** Transportation and wholesale trade.
- **Landmarks:** Interstate bus park, mechanic village, roadside market.
- **Economy:** Haulage, mechanics, wholesale, transport jobs.
- **Housing:** Dormitories, family compounds, cheap lodges.
- **Social flavour:** Gritty, practical, always in motion.
- **Instance density:** 64 players.

### Bower's Historic — *The City Remembers*
- **Character:** Heritage, low-income, proud.
- **Landmarks:** Bower's Tower monument, rust-roof neighbourhoods.
- **Economy:** Crafts, local trade, small services, tight-knit support.
- **Housing:** Family compounds, older homes, community spaces.
- **Social flavour:** Deep roots, strong community, watchful, proud.
- **Instance density:** 32 players.

### Agodi Green Zone — *Power and Leisure*
- **Character:** Administrative and recreational.
- **Landmarks:** Secretariat Complex, Agodi Lake Park, Golf Club.
- **Economy:** Civil service, recreation, events, premium leisure.
- **Housing:** Official quarters, green-zone homes.
- **Social flavour:** Calm, ordered, influential.
- **Instance density:** 32 players.

---

## 3. District Connections

Districts link by **roads, danfo routes, and paths**. Travel between them is real movement — time, cost, and encounter. A journey from UI Student Hub to Bodija Market is part of the game, not a loading screen.

---

## 4. District Identity Rules

1. Each district must be **recognisable in 30 seconds** of walking.
2. Each district has **at least one landmark** players can name.
3. Each district offers **distinct housing and earning** opportunities.
4. Reputation is **district-specific** — known here, unknown there.
