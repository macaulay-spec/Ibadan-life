> **North Star:** a city with a rhythm — day and night change how Ibadan lives, works, earns, and feels.

---

## 1. Why Time Matters

Time is not decoration. It **drives behaviour**: markets open and close, buses fill and thin, suya comes out at dusk, nightlife wakes in Bodija, and players need rest. A living city has a **pulse**.

---

## 2. The Day/Night Cycle

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Cycle length** | ~30 real minutes for a full day (tunable) |
| **Phases** | Dawn → Day → Dusk → Night |
| **Persistence** | Time continues while a player is offline (the city lives on) |

---

## 3. What Changes by Phase

| Phase | City behaviour | Player impact |
|-------|----------------|---------------|
| **Dawn** | Markets stir; buses fill; workers move | Fresh stock; busy transport; good earning |
| **Day** | Offices, campuses, shops, services open | Work shifts, errands, commerce, study |
| **Dusk** | Rush hour; street food (suya) appears; lights begin | Social hour; food needs; travel home |
| **Night** | Nightlife (Bodija), quiet residential, some crime risk | Fun/social needs; risk/reward; rest is safer at home |

---

## 4. Calendar & Events

- A simple **calendar** (days, weeks, months) drives rent cycles, wages, and events.
- **Weekly rhythms:** market days, owambe celebrations, community gatherings.
- **Seasonal/seasonal-feel events** support the living city (see LIVE_OPERATIONS.md) without replacing player agency.

---

## 5. Time & Needs

- **Energy** drains through the day; **rest at home** restores it.
- Working at night pays more but is riskier.
- Staying up too long degrades performance (a soft pressure, not a punishment).

---

## 6. Presentation

- Warm golden **daylight**; long shadows at dusk.
- **Streetlights, shop signs, and headlights** at night — the city glows.
- Audio shifts with time: day bustle, night quiet, dawn birds.
- All tuned for **mobile performance** (baked lighting, cheap effects).
