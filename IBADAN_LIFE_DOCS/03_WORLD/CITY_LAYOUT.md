> **North Star:** how the city is organised — its arteries, districts, and the way a player reads space.

---

## 1. Layout Philosophy

The city is laid out like a **real Nigerian city**: a hierarchy of **major roads, district centres, neighbourhood streets, and back alleys**, with landmarks that orient you. Navigation should feel like **knowing your way around**, not reading a map.

---

## 2. The Spatial Hierarchy

| Layer | Description | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| **Arterial roads** | Major routes connecting districts; danfo corridors | Ring Road, Iwo Road |
| **District centres** | The heart of each district; landmarks, markets, hubs | Bodija Market, Dugbe Mall |
| **Neighbourhood streets** | Residential and local commercial streets | Hostel rows, estate streets |
| **Alleys & paths** | Shortcuts, back routes, informal passages | Market alleys, campus paths |
| **Interiors** | Enterable buildings: homes, shops, bukas, banks, offices | A buka, a bank, a hostel room |

---

## 3. District Placement (Conceptual Map)

```
                 [Agodi Green Zone]
                        |
   [Bower's Historic]—[Dugbe CBD]—[Bodija Central]
                        |
                 [Iwo Transit Hub]
                        |
              [UI Student Hub]  ← Vertical Slice home
```

- **Dugbe CBD** sits centrally — the commercial spine.
- **Iwo Transit Hub** anchors movement and trade routes.
- **UI Student Hub** is the dense, affordable entry point (Vertical Slice).
- **Bodija Central** is the upscale market/residential quarter.
- **Bower's Historic** holds heritage and community.
- **Agodi Green Zone** is the calm administrative/recreational north.

---

## 4. Landmarks as Navigation

Players navigate by **landmarks**, not coordinates:

- *"Meet me at the UI Main Gate."*
- *"Behind Cocoa House."*
- *"Bodija Market, near the nightclub."*
- *"By the mechanic village, Iwo Road."*
- *"Bower's Tower, turn left."*

Every district must have **named, visible landmarks** that support this.

---

## 5. Movement & Flow

- **On foot:** pavements, crossings, alleys, stairs.
- **Public transport:** danfo and micra routes along arterials; okada and keke for short hops.
- **Private vehicles:** cars, vans, bikes owned or driven by players.
- **Flow matters:** congestion, traffic, and chokepoints are *features* — they create encounters and stories.

---

## 6. Scale & Performance

The layout is built with **World Partition** tiles and streamed for mobile. Detail is concentrated where players gather (district centres, markets, hubs); edges are lighter. The layout must never make a mid-range phone struggle.
