---

## 1. Philosophy

Jobs are **not** timer-based menu clicks. Work happens **in the world**: you drive, you serve, you cook, you fix, you guard, you sell. Your skill decides your pay; your reputation decides your access; other players are your employers, customers, and colleagues.

## 3. How Work Works

1. **Get hired** — by a player-owned business, a player employer, or a city service.
2. **Show up** — physically go to the workplace (presence matters).
3. **Work** — perform the job's activity (drive a route, serve tables, fix a car).
4. **Get paid** — wages in Naira, from the employer's till (server-validated).
5. **Grow** — skill rises with work; better jobs unlock.

---

## 4. Wages & Progression

- **Entry wages** are modest (a Lapo can always start).
- **Skill-gated tiers:** better skill → better pay and roles.
- **Reputation-gated roles:** trusted players get bigger responsibility (and risk).
- Careers have **natural ceilings** — mastery opens **ownership** (start your own business).

---

## 5. Player Employers

- Businesses **hire real players** for shifts (staffing is interdependence).
- A good employer builds a crew and a reputation; a bad one loses staff.
- **NPC fallback** exists for essential services only, and is clearly secondary.

---

## 6. Unemployment & Recovery

- Between jobs, players can hustle (small gigs, relief stipends for new Lapo players).
- **No dead ends:** there is always a way to earn the next meal and the next rent.

---

## 7. Rules

- Wages and employment are **server-authoritative**.
- Work requires **physical presence** (no remote menu labour).
- Careers serve **Believable Life Progression** — work should feel like a life path.
