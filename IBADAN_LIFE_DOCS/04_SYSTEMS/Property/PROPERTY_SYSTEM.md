> **North Star:** a place to call your own — housing that is a home, a base, a status, and a safe harbour in the city.

---

## 1. Philosophy

A home is more than shelter. It is where you **log off safely**, **restore needs**, **store your things**, and **show who you are**. Owning property is a core life milestone and a visible mark of progress.

---

## 2. Property Types

| Type | Role | Example |
|------|------|---------|
| **Hostel / Room** | Cheap entry housing | A small room in UI Student Hub |
| **Apartment / Flat** | Standard family housing | A flat in Dugbe or Bodija |
| **Family Compound** | Community living | A compound in Bower's Historic |
| **Gated Estate Home** | Upscale housing | A home in a Bodija estate |
| **Storefront** | Business property | A shop in Bodija Market |
| **Commercial / Office** | Business & professional | An office in Dugbe CBD |

---

## 3. Renting & Owning

- **Rent:** pay a recurring lease (weekly/monthly) to a landlord (player or the city).
- **Own:** buy a property outright for a large Naira sum; then pay **utilities and maintenance**.
- **Landlords** can be **real players** who earn rent — another thread of interdependence.

---

## 4. What a Home Does

| Function | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| **Safe logout** | Log off at home; resume exactly there |
| **Needs recovery** | Sleep restores Energy; a good home restores faster |
| **Storage** | Keep your items safe (see INVENTORY_AND_ITEM_SYSTEM.md) |
| **Social space** | Host friends, crew, gatherings |
| **Status** | Your address is visible; it says who you are |
| **Business base** | Some properties double as storefronts |

---

## 5. Utilities & Upkeep

- **Electricity** and **generator diesel** bills (the reality of power) — a recurring **sink**.
- **Maintenance** costs keep a property in good condition.
- Neglect degrades **recovery, storage, and reputation** — take care of your home.

---

## 6. Housing & the City

- Housing exists **per district**, with prices reflecting the area (Bodija costs more than a hostel).
- Scarcity and pricing respond to **population and demand**.
- A player can own **more than one** property (a home + a storefront).

---

## 7. Rules

- The **server** owns all ownership and rent records.
- Eviction and debt have **recovery paths** — losing a home is a setback, not an ending.
- Property never grants **power** over others; it grants **comfort, storage, and status**.
