> **North Star:** players build real businesses that need other players — to supply, staff, serve, and buy — so ownership is genuinely interdependent.

---

## 1. Philosophy

A business is a **player-owned enterprise** in the world. It has a **storefront**, a **supply chain**, **staff** (real players), and **customers** (real players). Owning one is a major life milestone — and a genuine responsibility.

---

|------|----------|-------------------|

| **Retail / Shop** | Provisions store, clothing, electronics | Retail |

| **Transport** | Danfo/okada/keke operator, haulage | Logistics |
| **Entertainment** | Nightclub, event promoter, games spot | Service |
| **Professional** | Bank, real estate, corporate office | Service |

---

## 3. Starting & Owning a Business

1. **Acquire a storefront** (rent or buy a property — see PROPERTY_SYSTEM.md).
2. **Register the business** (name, type, signage).
3. **Stock and supply** (buy from the supply chain or produce).
4. **Hire staff** (real players take jobs — see JOB_AND_CAREER_SYSTEM.md).
5. **Open and serve** customers (real players).
6. **Earn, pay costs, reinvest, grow.**

---

## 4. Business Operations

| Function | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| **Storefront** | A physical, visible, enterable shop in the world |
| **Inventory** | Stock bought from suppliers or the marketplace |
| **Staffing** | Hire players for shifts; they earn wages |
| **Pricing** | Owner sets prices within market floors/ceilings |
| **Revenue** | Sales to players; tracked by the server ledger |
| **Costs** | Rent, utilities, stock, wages, maintenance |
| **Reputation** | Good service builds standing; poor service spreads |

---

## 5. Customisation & Visibility

- **Signage and façade** customisation (neon signs, paint) increases visibility.
- A well-known, well-run shop becomes a **landmark** — a place people tell each other to meet.
- Customisation is **cosmetic and visibility-based**, never pay-to-win.

---

## 6. Business Health

- A business lives or dies by **real player traffic and supply**.
- Empty streets → no customers. Bad supply → empty shelves. Poor wages → staff leave.
- **Owning a business is a story of interdependence**, not a passive income faucet.

---

## 7. Rules

- Only **real players** are customers, staff, and suppliers (NPC fallback is limited and clearly secondary).
- The **server** validates all business transactions.
- A business can **fail** — and that failure is a story, with recovery paths.
