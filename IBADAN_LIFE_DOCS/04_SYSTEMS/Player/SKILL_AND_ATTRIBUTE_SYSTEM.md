> **North Star:** get better by doing — skills and attributes that grow with real work, and open real doors.

---

## 1. Philosophy

**You improve by doing.** Work a shift, you get better at the job. Drive a route, you drive it better. Skills and attributes grow through **play**, not timers or purchases.

---

## 2. Attributes (The Foundation)

Core attributes that shape what you can do and how well:

| Attribute | Governs |
|-----------|---------|
| **Stamina** | How long you can work, run, or stay active |
| **Charisma** | Social success — persuasion, first impressions, reputation |
| **Craft** | Quality of making, fixing, cooking, building |
| **Focus** | Precision work — driving, delicate tasks, study |
| **Hustle** | Finding and seizing opportunity; side-gig success |

Attributes grow slowly through **relevant activity** and are influenced by **needs and rest**.

---

## 3. Skills (The Paths)

Skills are **career- and life-specific** and gate progression:

| Skill | Path | Unlocks |
|-------|------|---------|
| **Driving** | Transport | Better vehicles, haulage, higher fares |
| **Cooking** | Food & service | Better dishes, restaurant ownership |
| **Mechanics** | Craft & repair | Better repairs, garage ownership |
| **Sales** | Trade & retail | Better deals, bigger shops |
| **Corporate** | Professional | Executive roles, offices |
| **Security** | Safety & order | Police/security roles, authority |

---

## 4. Growth Model

- **Practice → growth.** Doing the activity raises the skill.
- **Diminishing returns, no hard cap.** Mastery is a horizon.
- **Better skill → better pay, roles, and opportunities.**
- Skills are **persisted** and visible (your reputation for a craft).

---

## 5. Progression Integration

- Skills + attributes feed **careers** (JOB_AND_CAREER_SYSTEM.md).
- They shape **earning potential** and **business success**.
- They contribute to **reputation** (REPUTATION_SYSTEM.md).

---

## 6. Rules

1. **Growth by doing.** No paid shortcuts to skill.
2. **No single meta.** Many skills, many viable lives.
3. **Visible progress.** Players feel themselves getting better.
4. **Rest matters.** Attributes recover with sleep and care — life sim in the numbers.
