> **North Star:** your name means something — a reputation, earned district by district and circle by circle, that opens doors and carries consequences.

---

## 1. Philosophy

In a real city, **people know you**. Reputation is how the city remembers you — and it is **earned by consistent action**, not bought. It is **local**: famous in Dugbe, unknown in Bodija.

---

## 2. Reputation Is Local

Reputation is tracked **per district** and **per circle**:

- **District reputation:** how the neighbourhood sees you (UI Student Hub, Dugbe, Bodija, Iwo, Bower's, Agodi).
- **Circle reputation:** how a group sees you (a market, a campus, a crew, a profession).

You can be **loved in one place and unknown in another** — like a real city.

---

## 3. What Builds Reputation

| Action | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Pay debts on time** | Trust rises |
| **Keep promises / deliver** | Reliability rises |
| **Good service (business)** | Standing rises |
| **Help your community** | Local love rises |
| **Show up, be consistent** | Recognition rises |

## 4. What Damages Reputation

| Action | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Break promises / default** | Trust falls |
| **Poor service / bad goods** | Standing falls |
| **Crime (fictional)** | Notoriety rises; trust falls |
| **Scamming / exploiting players** | Reputation collapses; moderation follows |

---

## 5. What Reputation Unlocks

- **Better jobs** (employers trust you).
- **Better housing** (landlords trust you).
- **Bigger deals** (partners trust you).
- **Deeper trust** (friends, crew, access).

And it is **visible**: others can see your standing — **people recognise you**.

---

## 6. Reputation vs. Wanted Level

- **Reputation** is social standing — how people *respect* you.
- **Wanted level** is legal notoriety — how the law *watches* you.
- A player can be **respected and clean**, **respected and wanted**, or **unknown**. All are stories.

---

## 7. Rules

1. Reputation is **earned by action**, never purchased.
2. It is **local and contextual** (district/circle).
3. It is **visible** — the city knows your name.
4. **Recovery is possible.** A fall is a story; standing can be rebuilt.
