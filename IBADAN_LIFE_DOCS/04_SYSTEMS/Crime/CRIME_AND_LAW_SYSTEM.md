---

## 1. Philosophy

Crime exists as **one system among many**, with **meaningful consequences** — not as the main loop, and **never** as real-world instruction. The fantasy is **risk and consequence**, not glorified violence.

---

## 2. The Wanted Level

A player's **notoriety** (0 → 5 stars) triggers police response:

| Level | Response |
|-------|----------|
| **0 — Clean** | No attention |
| **1–2 — Known** | Warnings, fines, police stops |
| **3–4 — Wanted** | Active pursuit, roadblocks, arrest risk |
| **5 — Infamous** | City-wide response; serious consequences |

- Wanted level **decays** over time (lay low, leave town, pay it off).
- **Reputation is separate from wanted level** — a known hustler is not automatically a criminal.

---

## 3. Fictional Crimes (Examples)

- **Petty theft** (pickpocketing, shoplifting) — low risk, low reward.
- **Robbery / extortion** — higher risk, higher reward, real danger.
- **Smuggling / fencing goods** — supply-chain crime with logistics.
- **Corruption / bribery** — social and civic consequences.
- **Territory / crew conflict** — organised, high-stakes, story-rich.

All crimes are **fictional game mechanics**. No real-world criminal instruction is provided.

---

## 4. Law & Consequences

| Consequence | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **Fines** | Naira penalties; can be worked off |
| **Confiscation** | Goods or vehicles seized |
| **Arrest / jail** | Temporary removal from the streets; a story, not an ending |
| **Criminal reputation** | The city remembers; doors close and open |
| **Escalation** | Repeat offences raise the wanted level |

---

## 5. Police (NPC + Player)

- **NPC police** (traffic/law enforcement) are infrastructure — they exist so the law functions.
- **Player police / security roles** let real players hold order (a career with authority and responsibility).
- Chases, stops, and arrests are **physical, in-world events** — presence, not menus.

---

## 6. Design Rules

1. **Crime is optional.** The honest path is always viable and rewarding.
2. **Consequences are real** within the fiction — crime is a risk, not a shortcut.
3. **Recovery exists.** Jail, fines, and notoriety can be answered; no permanent deletion.
4. **No real-world instruction.** All crime is abstracted and fictionalised.
5. **Safety first.** Moderation tools address real-world harm (see MODERATION_AND_SAFETY.md).
